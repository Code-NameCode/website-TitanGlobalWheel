Frequently asked

Off-highway wheels, tyres and runflats — questions answered

Quick answers for fleet managers, procurement teams and engineers sourcing off-highway wheel systems in South Africa and sub-Saharan Africa.

What does Titan Global Wheel manufacture?

We design, test and manufacture off-highway tyres, steel and aluminium rims, Tyron runflat systems (inserts and bead locks), and wheel accessories such as studs and CTIS. We supply complete tyre–rim–runflat assemblies for agriculture, mining, military and construction — plus an in-house OTR rim repair workshop.

Where is Titan Global Wheel based, and where do you deliver?

We are based in South Africa and supply the whole of sub-Saharan Africa, with a strong presence in the SADC region's agriculture and mining sectors. Contact our sales team for export enquiries outside the region.

Is Titan Global Wheel ISO 9001 and B-BBEE certified?

Yes. Our design, testing and production facilities are ISO 9001 certified and we are a B-BBEE certified supplier. Both certificates are available to download from the footer of this page for procurement and tender use.

Do you repair and re-certify OTR rims?

Yes. Our ISO-certified workshop repairs, NDT-inspects, pressure-tests and re-certifies OTR rims from 25″ to 63″ for mining, earthmoving and agricultural fleets, at a fraction of the cost of a new rim.

What is the largest rim size you manufacture for agriculture?

We manufacture agricultural wheel assemblies up to 54″ in diameter - the largest in the region - covering tractors, harvesters, implements and irrigation equipment. Our steel and aluminium rims are available across the full Goodyear agricultural range, including the new NAMA line.

How do I request a quote for off-highway wheels, rims or runflats?

Use the contact form or call our sales team with your machine make/model, application, operating terrain, and the rim and tyre sizes you need. We will spec a complete assembly and quote it back the same week.
