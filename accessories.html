Accessories

Wheel accessories built for every application

High-quality wheel components, rim audits and site severity studies — everything you need to keep your equipment rolling safely and efficiently.

Overview

Quality Wheel Components

Wheel accessories are necessary to enable proper functioning and, just like steel rims, can be different to the equipment type. At Titan Global Wheel we fit high quality wheel components specified to the rim and tyre — ensuring safety, longevity and performance across all applications.

Value-Added Services

Beyond Components

Our technical expertise extends beyond product supply. We partner with our customers to maximise equipment uptime and wheel life through preventative inspections and site assessments.

Rim Audits

Our trained personnel visit your facility to visually inspect every wheel. Checks include lock rings, gutter-to-lock-ring gap, flange condition, bead seat bands, stud integrity and lateral wear measurement.

Site Severity Studies

Quarterly studies for mines to assess all conditions that may damage wheels — pit area condition, road surfaces, debris, loading zones and dump areas. Our technical teams identify issues before they cause downtime.

Troubleshooting & Support

Our technical teams assist customers on site in troubleshooting wheel conditions, identifying damage causes and recommending corrective actions to extend wheel service life.
