Overview

Tyron Easyfit Runflats

Tyron Easyfit Runflats are specialized safety systems designed to keep vehicles moving in the event of a tyre puncture, blowout, or other damage that would normally render a tyre inoperable. These systems are typically used in critical vehicles such as security, emergency, military, and armored vehicles, as well as certain commercial applications.

These runflats are a crucial safety feature for vehicles operating in environments with a high risk of road hazards, including off-road terrains, hostile areas, or regions prone to deliberate tyre-spiking incidents.

Features

Key Features

The Tyron Easyfit system combines innovative engineering with rugged materials to deliver reliable runflat performance in the harshest conditions.

  • Safety Assurance They prevent a complete loss of control after a puncture or blowout, allowing the driver to steer the vehicle safely to a stop or a secure location.
  • Multi-Piece Design The Easyfit system consists of a multi-piece construction that can be installed on most standard wheels without requiring specialized tools.
  • Durable Materials These runflats are constructed from high-strength materials like polymer alloys, ensuring durability and resistance to extreme conditions.
  • Bead Locking Tyron systems often include beadlock functionality, which helps keep the tyre secured to the wheel even when operating at low or zero pressure.
  • Compliance & Standards They are tested and compliant with international standards, ensuring reliability in demanding situations.
