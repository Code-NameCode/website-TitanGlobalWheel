Maintenance and Care Tips :

Keeping your LSW tyres from Titan in top condition is essential to ensure maximum performance and longevity. Here are some maintenance and care tips to help you get the most out of your LSW tyres:

1. Regular Inspection

It is important to inspect your LSW tyres regularly to ensure they are in good condition. Check for any cuts, punctures, or signs of wear and tear. If you notice any issues, it is best to get them repaired or replaced as soon as possible to avoid any further damage.

2. Proper Inflation

Maintaining the correct inflation pressure is crucial for the optimal performance of your LSW tyres. Overinflation can cause uneven wear and reduce the tyre's lifespan, while underinflation can lead to increased rolling resistance and decreased fuel efficiency. Make sure to check the inflation pressure regularly and adjust it as necessary.

3. Proper Storage

When storing your LSW tyres, make sure to keep them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Avoid stacking them too high or placing heavy objects on top of them, as this can cause damage to the tyres.

4. Regular Cleaning

Keeping your LSW tyres clean can help prevent the buildup of dirt and debris, which can cause damage over time. Use a mild soap and water to clean the tyres, and avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive materials that can damage the tyre's surface. By following these maintenance and care tips, you can ensure that your LSW tyres from Titan remain in top condition and deliver optimal performance for years to come.
