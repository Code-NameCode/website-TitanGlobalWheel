Steel & aluminium rims, designed & tested in-house

For over 40 years Titan Global Wheel has supported passive applications for defence forces around the world. Our niche is the ability to design and test specific steel and aluminium rim requirements and manufacture small to large batch quantities — providing customers with flexibility and shorter lead times.

Complete tyre, rim & run flat insert assemblies

Titan Global Wheel is the Tyron Run Flat distributor in Africa. Together with various military tyre manufacturers, this alliance enables us to supply a complete tyre, rim and run flat insert assembly. Tyron's patented modular system is field mountable and demountable without a hydraulic press — the only one of its kind in the world.
