Tyres & Run Flats

Tyres matched to the mission

Our strengthened partnership with Tyron Run Flat Systems makes us their distributor of Run Flat Inserts in Africa. Together with various military tyre manufacturers, this alliance enables us to supply a complete tyre, rim and run flat insert assembly. Tyron's patented modular system is field mountable and demountable without a hydraulic press — the only one of its kind in the world.

  • Tyron run flat distributor in Africa
  • Military tyre manufacturers
  • Complete tyre, rim & RFI assembly
  • Patented modular design
  • Field mountable & demountable
  • No hydraulic press required
