What we repair

Full-scope OTR wheel repair — sandblasting to certification

Four integrated services, run on-site in our Johannesburg facility. We carry replacement components and parts to keep the repair process efficient.

Sandblasting & Inspection

Every wheel is sandblasted to bare metal so the full defect picture is visible — no hidden cracks left behind paint.

  • OEM-standard defect reporting with photos
  • Fixed quote before any work commences
  • Full dimensional check against OEM specs

OEM-spec Welding & Re-engineering

Certified welders re-engineer cracked, gouged or worn rim sections back to OEM geometry using approved procedures.

  • Approved welding procedure specifications
  • Replacement flanges, lock rings & gutter profiles in stock
  • Certified welders with full weld maps

MPI & Ultrasonic Testing

Final non-destructive testing on every wheel — Magnetic Particle Inspection on smaller wheels and Ultrasonic testing on every circumferential weld on Super Giant rims.

  • MPI on smaller OTR wheels
  • Ultrasonic testing on Super Giant welds
  • Technicians certified to industry standards

Refurbishment & Repaint

After welding and testing, every wheel is repainted to OEM colour and finish, then sealed against corrosion for the long haul.

  • OEM colour match on request
  • Anti-corrosion sealing on all surfaces
  • Final fitment check before sign-off

Frequently asked

Answers before you book the collection

Which OTR wheel sizes can Titan Global Wheel repair?
We repair everything from mid-size loader and ADT wheels up to 57″ and 63″ IGLR Super Giant wheels used on the largest rigid-frame haul trucks. Our repair lines are set up for both light- and heavy-duty rims found across mining, quarrying, construction and earthmoving fleets.
What inspection and testing do you perform before and after the repair?
Every wheel is sandblasted to bare metal, then inspected against OEM standards using Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI) on smaller wheels and Ultrasonic Testing on every circumferential weld on Super Giant wheels. A detailed defect report with photos and a fixed quote is presented to the customer before any welding begins.
What warranty do you offer on a repaired OTR rim?
Every repaired rim carries a 6-month warranty, and a Certificate of Conformance is issued on completion. Final inspection includes MPI on smaller wheels or ultrasonic testing on Super Giant circumferential welds to confirm compliance with industry standards.
Do you handle collection and delivery of OTR wheels across Southern Africa?
Yes. We arrange collection and delivery across Southern Africa for customers with a fleet of OTR wheels and runflats. Our logistics network covers South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, Zimbabwe, Zambia, Mozambique, DRC and Tanzania for haul-truck and loader fleets.

Got a wheel that needs work? Get the defect report first.

Send us the wheel serial, machine model and a quick photo of the damage. We'll arrange collection, sandblast and inspect on arrival, then send you a photo-backed defect report and a fixed quote — before any welding starts.
