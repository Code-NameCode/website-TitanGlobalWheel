Who we are

One supplier. The complete wheel system.

Titan Global Wheel is one of the largest combined manufacturers and suppliers of commercial, mining, agricultural, earthmoving and military wheels in sub-Saharan Africa. We don't sell parts - we engineer complete, battle-proven assemblies that work as one system.

From the family farm to the front line, our wheels are trusted wherever the work is hard and failure is not an option. Through ISO-certified design, testing and production facilities, we deliver shorter lead times, custom steel and aluminium rim specifications, and the after-sales support of a dealer network that reaches nearly any machine, wherever it operates.

As the exclusive African distributor and licensee of the Goodyear agricultural tyre brand, we pair world-class technology with deep local knowledge - so the gear that feeds, builds and defends the continent simply keeps turning.

Our journey

Six decades of wheel engineering

Milestones that built Titan Global Wheel into sub-Saharan Africa's leading off-highway wheel manufacturer.

  • 1960s

    Guestro Wheels

    Having started out as a rim manufacturer in 1965, the original Guestro Wheels business was a part of the Dorbyl Group.

  • 1980s

    Foundations in off-highway wheels

    Beginning decades of specialist design and supply for the agricultural and commercial sectors across Southern Africa.

  • 1990s

    Defence & runflat expertise

    40+ years of supporting defence forces begins - building the relationships and capability behind our military rims and runflat systems.

  • 2000s

    Mining & earthmoving reach

    A strong, sustained presence at major Southern African mining sites, with heavy-load rims and tyres built to last.

  • 2010s

    Global Wheel & 100% Local

    In 2012 our name changed to Global Wheel, the company became 100% local once again, majority Black owned and achieved a level 2 B-BBEE status.

  • Today

    #1 in sub-Saharan Africa

    Exclusive Tyron distributor, Goodyear agricultural licensee, and the continent's largest combined off-highway wheel manufacturer.

One of the largest combined manufacturers and suppliers of commercial, mining, agricultural, earthmoving and military wheels in sub-Saharan Africa.
