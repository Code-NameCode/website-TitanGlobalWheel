The partnership

The Goodyear Agri licence, explained

What the licence means, where the tyres come from, and why it matters for farmers across the region.

Exclusive licensee

The exclusive African distributor and licensee of the Goodyear agricultural tyre brand, and the official source for Goodyear Farm Tires in South Africa and sub-Saharan Africa.

Goodyear brand, Titan-made

The Goodyear agricultural tyre brand is owned by Titan International, and Goodyear Farm Tires are made by Titan. Through the licence, that technology is reintroduced to local and regional markets.

Locally supported

Stock lands on SA shores and is backed by our Heidelberg-based team, with a dealer network that reaches nearly any machine, wherever it operates.

Complete wheel systems

Goodyear agricultural tyres mounted on our own rims, from 18" to 54", delivered as matched, ready-to-fit wheel assemblies rather than loose parts.

Why it matters

World-class technology, home-soil support

A world-famous name in farm tyres, backed by a sub-Saharan manufacturer that specs, supplies and supports the complete wheel.

  • ✓ Less soil compaction and lower input costs, season after season
  • ✓ Radial and bias-ply options for tractors, harvesters, forestry and irrigation
  • ✓ Tyres supplied mounted on Titan-engineered rims as one system
  • ✓ Local stock, local warranty support and nationwide dealer reach
The range

Goodyear agricultural tyres we supply

A full line-up for tractors, harvesters, forestry, implements and irrigation. Radial and bias-ply, high-flotation and row-crop.

01

Goodyear LSW®

Low-Sidewall technology: a bigger wheel diameter with a shorter sidewall for less soil compaction, improved stability, and the solution to harvesting wet and muddy fields.

02

DYNATORQ & RIB TRACTOR

Bias-ply drive tyres with deep lugs and durable compounds, engineered for dependable traction and long wear on tractors and implements.

03

OPTITRAC

Radial tyres built for modern high-horsepower tractors: comfort, road performance and fuel efficiency without giving up field traction.

04

Hybrid R14 (R1 + R4)

The new Hybrid R14 pairs R1 agricultural performance with R4 industrial durability for exceptional stability and road performance.

05

High-flotation & irrigation

Wide-footprint flotation tyres that protect delicate soils while carrying pivots, spreaders and harvesters across soft ground.

06

Fitted on Titan rims

Every Goodyear agri tyre can be supplied mounted on Titan-engineered rims as a complete, matched wheel assembly, ready for the field.
