Engineered for the bite

Six engineering features. One unstoppable tyre.

Every block, sipe and shoulder lug on the NAMA X/T has a job. Together, they give you the grip of an off-road tyre and the manners of a highway tyre.

FEATURE 01

Wider running surface

More rubber in contact with the road means more stability, more traction, and a more confident feel under load — on the tar and on the dirt.

FEATURE 02

3D siping

Multi-directional sipes that bite into the surface for grip, traction and better control when braking and turning — wet or dry.

FEATURE 03

Right-angle shoulders

Square shoulders widen the contact patch at the edge of the tread and give the X/T a bolder, more aggressive stance on the road.

FEATURE 04

Stone ejectors

Self-cleaning grooves that work stones and debris out of the tread as you drive — keeping your traction consistent, kilometre after kilometre.

FEATURE 05

Staggered shoulder lugs

Outer-shoulder lugs grab the dirt; the staggered pattern also improves water drainage and heat dissipation under heavy braking.

FEATURE 06

Dual sidewall

Two sidewall designs moulded into one tyre. Flip the wheel on the rim to switch from street-clean to off-road-bold — no extra cost, no compromise.
