44 Years!

Titan Global Wheel7 May 20262 min read

Last Thursday, we celebrated two incredible members of the Titan Global Wheel family as they began their well-deserved retirement journeys.

After an amazing 44 years of dedication, Irene leaves behind a legacy of commitment, hard work, and countless memories that have shaped our company through the decades.

We also bid farewell to Brakhi, who has dedicated 27 years of loyal service and has been an important part of the Titan Global Wheel team and culture.

Your years of service, passion, and contribution will never be forgotten. Thank you both for everything you have done for Titan Global Wheel.

We wish you many years of happiness, relaxation, and new adventures ahead. 💙
